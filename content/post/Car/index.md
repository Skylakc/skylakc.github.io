I bought a 2003 Volkswagen Golf MK4 1.8L, naturally aspirated. By the time it reached me, it had already passed through twelve owners.

After an accident, I had to repair it.

The car is built on the same platform as the Audi TT MK1, named PQ34.

As a result, I used a variety of processes to modify my car, including but not limited to adding a new turbocharger.

## Teardown

### Engine

![](teardown.jpg)

![](head.jpg)

### ECU Program

I rebuilt the entire ECU, both hardware and software. The main MCU was replaced with an Infineon Aurix TC264 running at a higher frequency, which completely improves the sensor refresh rate and provides a high-speed interface for logging.

![](ECUs.jpg)
